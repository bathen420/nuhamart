# Changelog — v0.9.0 Professional Invoice System

## Added

- Professional A4 sales invoice.
- Direct DomPDF invoice download.
- Thermal receipt layouts for 58mm and 80mm printers.
- Reliable print preparation after fonts and page layout are ready.
- Company settings integration: name, tagline, logo, address, phone, email, website, currency and footer.
- Customer, cashier, payment and transaction details.
- Original, returned and net sale summary for returned sales.
- Invoice action buttons on the Sales Show page.
- Invoice route registration test.

## Safety

- No database migration is required.
- Existing sales and return data are not modified.
- Invoice output is read-only.
